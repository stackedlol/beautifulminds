For Beautiful Minds.

X is about to pay me now. Not a fortune, but enough to notice.

Every creator payout I get goes to research and therapies for BPD, schizophrenia, autism, ADHD and other neurodivergent minds: the funds that study them, the programs that make care like DBT reachable, and the people who help families through it.

These are still the conditions people whisper about. The research is underfunded, good therapy is hard to get, and too many people get written off. I want a part in changing that.

It doesn't have to be big. It doesn't have to be small. It has to be real.

Every dollar in and every dollar out goes on a public record, with the receipt. Anything I haven't given yet shows up as still to give, for everyone to see. Don't take my word for it. Check.

How it works.

01

Logged

Every payout from X and every donation goes on one public list. Each donation links to its receipt.

02

Sealed

Each line gets a fingerprint made from what it says plus the line before it. Change one old number and its fingerprint stops matching, so the edit shows.

03

Checked

A bot re-checks the whole list every day, in public. If a line was changed, the check fails where anyone can see it.
